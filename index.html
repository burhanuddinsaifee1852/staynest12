Frequently asked questions

When is my stay confirmed?

After owner acceptance and room allocation. You can follow the status of every request under My requests.

Are utilities included in the rent?

It depends on the property. Each stay page lists meals and electricity separately, so you can see what is included before you request.

Can I visit the property before moving in?

Yes. Use Schedule a visit on any stay page to suggest a date and time to the owner.

What documents are usually required?

Owners commonly ask for a photo ID and a college or employer ID. The owner will confirm the exact list when you connect.
